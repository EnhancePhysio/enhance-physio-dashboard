How does this work?

Recall Effectiveness is auto-calculated from sheets you add — total recalls % = completed ÷ due; conversion % = rebooked ÷ completed.

Other KPIs are manual entry per quarter. Values save as you change them.

A KPI group is "hit" when all its sub-metrics meet target. Each hit earns $375 for the quarter.

Quarters: Q1 Jan–Mar, Q2 Apr–Jun, Q3 Jul–Sep, Q4 Oct–Dec.

About the bonus calculation

$5,000/yr guaranteed + $375 per KPI hit per quarter, max $20,000/yr. 10 KPI groups × 4 quarters × $375 = $15,000 max performance.

A KPI group counts as "hit" only when all its sub-metrics meet target.
